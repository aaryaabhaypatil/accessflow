AccessFlow is a browser extension that empowers you to navigate websites using only your eyes. Built for accessibility, it provides a hands-free browsing experience, making the web more inclusive for people with lived experience of disability.
Key Features
  • Eye Gesture Navigation – Scroll, click, and explore the web with nothing but your gaze.
  • Customisable Gestures – Fine-tune sensitivity and actions to match your style.
  • Real-Time Tracking – Enjoy smooth, instant responses as your eyes lead the way.
  • Accessible by Design – Creating alongside people with lived experience of disability.
  • Privacy First – Your gaze data never leaves your browser. Ever.
  • Game-Based Calibration – Kick things off with fun mini-games that get your eyes in sync.
